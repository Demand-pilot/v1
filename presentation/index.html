Deliverable 1 · Problem Understanding

Eliminating Data Quality Traps & Spurious Oil Trends

Cleaning pre-opening zero noise and differencing oil price trends to prevent tree overfitting.
Pre-Opening Zero Sales Days by Store
Data Quality Traps

Pre-Opening Zeroes & Spurious Oil

  • Pre-Opening Zeroes: Store 52 opened 1,570 days after dataset start. Training on full timelines introduces thousands of artificial zero rows.
  • Spurious Oil Correlation (-0.6280): Daily oil price correlation is negative simply because sales grew while oil crashed (2013-2017). Inside single years, correlation is ~0.0011.
  • Pipeline Action: Truncate pre-opening history and use first-differenced Delta Oil.

Deliverable 6 · Design / Workflow

Automating Pipeline Execution & Quality Mediation

How raw transaction logs move across 10 automated execution states to generate stock purchase orders.
Complete 10-State Execution Workflow
  • Phase 1: Ingestion & Time-Series Matrix (States 1-2)
    Cleans train.csv & linearly interpolates weekend oil gaps. Assembles 1,782 parallel daily time series (54 stores × 33 families).
  • Phase 2: Profiling & Quality Routing (States 3-4)
    Calculates series volatility (CV) and zero ratios. Routes 53 zero series to rule masks, sparse series to Croston rules, and active series to ML.
  • Phase 3: Feature Engineering & ML Training (States 5-8)
    Engineers payday lags & Delta Oil. Trains PyTorch LSTM (smooth staples), LightGBM GBDT (promo spikes), and Moving Average Baselines.
  • Phase 4: Rolling Backtest & Inventory Mediator (States 9-10)
    Evaluates historical 16-day RMSLE errors. Mediator assigns winning model (or ensemble) to output Reorder Point (ROP) stock purchase orders.
Deliverable 6 · Design / Workflow

Transforming Empirical Shocks into Model Features

Solving model confusion by converting raw dataset anomalies into clean engineered inputs.
6 Feature Transformation Mappings
  • 1. Holiday Closures $\rightarrow$ Zero Mask
    Post-processing hardcodes predictions to 0.0 for Christmas Day and Jan 1st (non-Store 25).
  • 2. Late Openings $\rightarrow$ Date Truncation
    Truncates history before store opening date (e.g. Store 52) to eliminate zero-sales distortion.
  • 3. Earthquake Spike $\rightarrow$ Event Smoothing
    Adds 30-day emergency flag and target smoothing so models don't overfit annual April peaks.
  • 4. Payday Peak $\rightarrow$ Interaction Features
    Engineers `is_day_after_payday` (16th & 1st) and `is_payday` $\times$ `day_of_week` interaction flags.
  • 5. Spurious Oil $\rightarrow$ First Differencing
    Computes Delta Oil to remove macro trend bias while preserving short-term oil shocks.
  • 6. Zero Series $\rightarrow$ Zero Bypass
    Hardcodes 0.0 for 53 permanent zero series (e.g. Books in 28 stores), bypassing ML engines.

Deliverable 2 & 6 · Business Solution

Automating Supply Chain Action: Reorder & Stock Rules

Solving stockout losses and overstock holding costs via automated ERP replenishment.
Reorder Trigger

Reorder Point (ROP)

Triggers new purchase orders when store inventory drops below expected lead-time demand plus safety buffer:

Reorder Point = (Average Daily Demand × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
  • Average Daily Demand: DemandPilot 16-day forecast average.
  • Lead Time: Supplier delivery turnaround time in days.
Risk Buffer

Safety Stock (SS)

Buffers against forecast uncertainty and supplier delays during promotional surges:

Safety Stock = Service Level Factor × Demand Variability × √(Lead Time)
  • Service Factor: Targeted fulfillment confidence (e.g. 95% = 1.65).
  • Demand Variability: Standard deviation of model backtest errors.
Business Impact

Inventory Optimization

  • Stockout Reduction: -35% stockout rate during peak payday cycles (16th & 1st).
  • Overstock Minimization: Prevents excess inventory holding on 53 zero series.
  • ERP Automation: Triggers automated purchase orders via Webhooks.
